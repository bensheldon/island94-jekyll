title: Stop sensationalizing
tags: []

Using an app isn’t an experience. Forgetting something doesn’t mean there’s a gap. The essence isn’t a spine. Trying a few things and keeping what works doesn’t need to be hill climbing. Not every abstraction is a primitive, every improvement an unlock, every feedback loop a flywheel, every advantage a moat, or every change a paradigm shift. Not everything is 10x, AI-native, agentic, the missing layer, or the operating system for X. And we’re hardly ever cooked.

Let apps be apps, features be features, improvements be improvements, and problems be problems. Talk like the real person you are, and save the big words for the rare occasions when you actually need them.
